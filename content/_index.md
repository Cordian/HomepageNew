I am a professor of mathematics at [UiT The Arctic University of Norway](https://uit.no/startsida), where I lead the [Algebra group](https://uit.no/research/algebra) and co-direct the [Lie–Størmer Centre](https://uit.no/research/lsc). I also care about the place of mathematics in culture and society, and about mathematics education at every level.
